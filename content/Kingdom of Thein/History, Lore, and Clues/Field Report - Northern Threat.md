Research Division — Surface Observation Report  
Containment Network — [[Kingdom of Thein/History, Lore, and Clues/Clues/Iron Veins|Iron Veins]]
### SURFACE ACTIVITY
The extreme magic users continue to move south from the northern mountains. Their numbers remain uncertain.

Where they remain for extended periods, the land becomes dead. Vegetation withers. Animal populations disappear. Water sources become unreliable. In several locations, the effect has persisted long after the users have departed.

We have confirmed that the destruction is not caused by ordinary spellcraft.

The energy behaves differently. It remains in the land.

---
### PROTECTION MEASURES

Our current protective measures appear effective against brief exposure.
- Reinforced subterranean chambers reduce magical intrusion.
- Crystal arrays can detect significant fluctuations before they reach the city.
- The outer containment pylons appear to redirect or disperse concentrated magical energy.

Theory: Greater protection may be achieved by grounding the energy into the mountain itself.

Warning: Do not allow direct exposure to concentrated magical fields. Previous testing resulted in structural failures and loss of personnel.
### NORTHERN MOVEMENT

All observed groups have originated from the north. They are not traveling like an army. They appear to be searching.

For what, we do not know.

Their movements have become increasingly deliberate since the latest disturbance beneath the Frostspires.

If they continue south, they will eventually discover us.
