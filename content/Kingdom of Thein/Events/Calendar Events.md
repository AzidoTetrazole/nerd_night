Structure:

- 12 months (Jan–Dec)
- 30 days each
- 360-day year
- Seasons mirror North America
- Solstices & Equinoxes fall on the 15th of their seasonal midpoint months for clean symmetry

# 🌑 The Four Major Solstice Festivals
These are kingdom-wide observances. All cities participate.

---
## 1. Winter Solstice — Night of the Veil

December 15

Theme: Endurance, Silence, Survival  
Season: Deep Winter

- Longest night of the year.
- Fires are kept burning until dawn.
- Citizens leave lanterns in windows to “guide the lost.”
- In Nyvere, bells ring at midnight.
- In rural towns, people tell survival stories.

---
## 2. Spring Equinox — Bloomtide

March 15

Theme: Renewal, Forgiveness, Beginnings  
Season: Early Spring

- Debts forgiven (symbolically, not legally).
- New apprentices announced.
- Trees planted in town squares.
- Arcane academies hold open demonstrations.

---
## 3. Summer Solstice — Highsun Ascendant

June 15

Theme: Strength, Honor, Triumph  
Season: Midsummer
- Tournaments held in Tyr.
- Naval displays in Abyr.
- Public oaths renewed in Nyvere.
- Bonfires lit at dusk.

This is the most politically visible holiday.

---
## 4. Autumn Equinox — Harvest’s Vigil

September 15

Theme: Gratitude, Preparation, Balance  
Season: Early Fall

- Communal feasts.
- Grain blessings.
- Night markets.
- Offerings to the gods for winter protection.

# 🌒 Three Minor Holidays

Smaller observances celebrated regionally or by certain groups.

---

## 1. Founding Day

January 6

Marks the recorded founding of Thein by the First Men.

- Parades in Nyvere.
- Speeches about unity.
- Military displays in Tyr.
- Students enrollment day at Schools

---
## 2. Day of Quiet Waters

July 4

Mostly celebrated near Lake Aeloria and Lakeshadow.

- No boats launched.
- No fishing permitted.
- Bells rung at dusk.

---

## 3. The Emberfast

November 30

A day of restraint.

- No arcane magic cast publicly.
- Temples hold silent prayer.
- Spellcasters fast from casting if able.

Academies strongly encourage participation.

---
# 🏙 City-Specific Holidays

Each major city has one defining celebration.

---
## Nyvere — Crown’s Accord

April 20

Celebrates the unity between the Crown and the Hands (city leadership).

- Legal pardons announced.
- Public court sessions held.
- Oaths of office renewed.
- Grand feast in the capital square.

---
## Abyr — Tideforged Day

August 12

Celebrates Abyr’s naval dominance.

- Ship races.
- Harbor fireworks.
- Blessing of the fleet.
- Mock sea battles for spectacle.

Sailors pour a cup of rum into the sea “for those taken.”

---

## Tyr — Ironbound Muster

May 25

Celebrates Tyr’s military strength.

- City-wide weapons inspection.
- Arena trials. (jousting, swords, brawls, etc)
- Public enlistment ceremonies.

Prisoners may earn reduced sentences by competing.

---
## Lakeshadow - Mistfall Remembrance

October 10

- Moment of silence at dawn.
- No swimming.
- Lanterns floated at night.  

Officially for drowned sailors.
