Leodithas Queldirer - Wizard

Leodithas is a young elven man, with short cut black and silver hair. He is the youngest son of Lord Queldirer in Abyr (Trade/Agriculture/Navy). He was thought to be blind for the first years of his life. While he was not abused or physically neglected he sensed his parents disdain. Until his early teens his siblings were favored. As Leodithas grew, he found he wasn't blind, but his vision was overwhelmed with seeing beyond normal sight. As he learned to control his vision, he discovered his sight extended into the foundation of magic, as well as the future and past. He began to perceive magic, time, and potential as strings or strands that flow through the world.

Once his potential was discovered, he suddenly was given favor and attention by his parents and people of power. Now, no expense was spared in Leodithas's education and training. His vision gave him an advantage in learning magic and spellcasting under wizards in Abyr.

Recently, Leodithas has had a recurring dream/vision that greatly disturbed him. He believes he needs to gain as much power as possible to avoid the terrible potential of that vision, and thus joins the School of Adamantine. The School of Adamantine is known for its brutality, but its also known that those who graduate have greater power in Thein. Other schools may be easier, but this is the quickest way towards his goals.

When Leo announced his plans to join the School of Adamantine his parents have tried to convince him to go to another college but Leodithus is pig-headed enough he won't change his mind.

Personality

* Prideful /judgemental
* Fear of shame/failure - fears this could lead to loss of his improved station
* Has good intentions but wants to gain power by any means necessary

Relationships

* Parents - Rennyn (Father) and Ellarian (Mother) -  cold/resentful - feels only given favor because of his gift, was "ignored" before then. 
* Siblings

  * Neremyn (oldest brother) - most strained relationship. Neremyn attended the School of Cloaks and was assumed to take over the family business. He felt Leo was taking his rightful place as his abilities were prioritized, and is even more so now that Leo is “rejecting” the family.
  * Aleratha (older sister) - Closest relationship in the family, was kind when Leo was young and didn’t show resentment as he grew.

* Wizard masters - respectfully suspicious - thinks they were only interested after his gift was discovered

Hooks

* His recurring dream - finding elements from the vision in the real world, or the vision changing /becoming more clear
* Power - growing in power to maintain his pride
* True non-transactional relationships
