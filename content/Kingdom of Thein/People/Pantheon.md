# The Lower Powers

The people of Thein know these names — but only as cautionary figures.

They are treated as:

- Fallen beings
- Corruptors
- Distant threats
- Defeated adversaries

Not active forces. And certainly not equals to the gods.
## 🔥 Fulcir — God of the Damned, Lord of the Hells
Domain: Damnation, Contracts, Punishment, Order Through Suffering  
Alignment Flavor: Lawful Evil

Seat: The Iron Crucible (Layered Hells)

“Fulcir takes what the gods will not redeem.” - temple teachings
## ⛓ Granok — The Shattered Kin
Domain: Ruin, Broken Divinity, Vengeance  
Alignment Flavor: Lawful Neutral

Status: Imprisoned

Origin: Granok was not created.

He was born with the gods.

He is of their substance.

When the pantheon first shaped reality, Granok proposed something radical:

“Mortals must ascend. Let them grow beyond us.”

The other gods disagreed.

“Do not reach beyond your station, lest you fall like Granok.” - what parents tell children

## 🌀 Razalez — The First Chaos
Domain: Chaos, Mutation, Beasts, Fey  
Alignment Flavor: Chaotic Neutral / Alien  
Seat: The Verdant Fracture (Feywild)

The Creator of Monsters

History books say:

- The gods guided the First Men through peril.
- They were delivered to Thein for protection.
- Evil forces (Granok and his kin) were defeated.
- The gods secured safety and peace.

This is taught as divine benevolence.
