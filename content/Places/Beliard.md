A dusty waypoint in the Dessarin Valley, Beliard is a market-moot for local cattle drovers. It surrounds the intersection of the Dessarin Road with the Stone Trail.

Beliard is home to many cattle ranchers whose herds roam the hills around it, particularly to the east. The village offers a public well, as well as a
pond where harnessed horses or oxen can be driven through the water to bathe them, drive off flies, and let them drink. It’s also home to a tanner, a
smith, some horse dealers and trainers who keep extensive stables, and an inn: the venerable, popular, and several-times-expanded Watchful Knight.
