A Fire Genasi member of the [[Cult of the Eternal Flame|Cult of the Eternal Flame]]. He is claimed to be at [[Scarlet Moon Hall|Scarlet Moon Hall]]. He worked with the [[Bruldenthar|Bruldenthar]] and [[Rhundorth|Rhundorth]] of the Harpers to arrange the caravan from Mirabar.

[[Axel]] revealed that Bastian was involved in the deaths of his family, and Axel is seeking revenge!
