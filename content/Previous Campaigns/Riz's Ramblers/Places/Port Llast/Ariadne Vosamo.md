#### Profile
Friend of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Thelid]]
Resident of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Places/Port Llast/Port Llast]]


Ariadne Vosamo is the daughter of Kadik Vosamo , the militia leader based in Port Llast , and friend of Rizlyn and Thelid . During the chaos of the escape from Port Llast, Ariadne and Thelid escaped to Butterskull Ranch.

Ariadne was kidnapped and taken back to Port Llast, and nearly forced to marry [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Places/Port Llast/Haermos Dothwintyl]]. The Ramblers arrived in time to save her from her fate, and stop the invasion of mind-flayers.
