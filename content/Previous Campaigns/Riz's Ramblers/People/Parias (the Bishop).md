# Profile
Savior of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Njal Stormbringer Ragnarsson|Njal Stormbringer Ragnarsson]]
Father of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Nyven of Caulderon]]
Lover of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Triptan of Caulderon]]

A hardhar (warrior priest) of Tempus, who cared for Njal after a great battle, and guided him on his first steps in the service of Tempus. After the loss of Triptan, he swore revenge on [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/People/Koraboros]] and anyone who let her die.
