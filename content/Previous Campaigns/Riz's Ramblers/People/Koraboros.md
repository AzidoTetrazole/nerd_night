## Profile
Pit fiend bound by [[Ammon Jerro|Ammon Jerro]]. Connected to Lazuli through a pact, who then worked to release Koraboros. Released by the sacrifice of Triptan through the manipulations of Lazuli to force Triptans hand.

He was defeated and sent back to the pit by [[The Ramblers]]
