Thelid was held captive in Port Llast, and forced to be a warrior for the leader of the city. After escaping (with help from [[Nerd Night Campaign/a_NerdPrivate/DM Notes/Meta Campaign/PCs/Barakus]] and [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Rizlyn|Rizlyn]]), he accompanied his friend Ariadne to a farm near [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Places/Phandalin/Phandalin|Phandalin]]. After Rizlyn's death, Thelid joined the Ramblers.

As a member of the Ramblers, Thelid successfully defeated an incursion of mind flayers trying to make a foothold on the material plane in Port Llast. For his heroism, he was named a Free Lord.

Thelid fell in battle against a giant abomination. However, his name was written in the remaining dust from the [[Orb of Fate]], leaving his ultimate fate unknown.
