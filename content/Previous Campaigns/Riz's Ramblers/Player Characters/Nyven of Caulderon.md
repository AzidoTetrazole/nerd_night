## Profile
Founding member of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/The Ramblers]]

Daughter of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Triptan of Caulderon]]

Business partner of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Barakus Enigma]] in [[TT's Tower]]

Childhood friend of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/People/Lazuli of Caulderon]]

Raised in the volcanic town of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Places/Caulderon/Village of Caulderon]], Nyven grew up under the watchful eye of her mother [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Triptan of Caulderon]]. Her childhood best friend was [[People/Lazuli]]. 

When Lazuli fell under the sway of [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/People/Koraboros]], Nyven too was drawn into a pact with the pit fiend.

As part of the Ramblers, she chose to sacrifice herself to save [[Previous Campaigns/Riz's Ramblers/Player Characters/Barakus Enigma]] when he was in mortal danger, placing herself under the complete control of Koraboros. The Ramblers fought to free Nyven from his clutches, but only succeeded after the sacrifice of Triptan.
