Siblings were guards in Nightstone
Ambushed and Betrayed the [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/Legend of the Motley Few#The Motley Few|The Motley Few]]

These two adventurers had siblings among the guards of Nightstone. Due to the Motley Few siding with the Zentarim and killing the guards, Godrick and Keladry swore vengeance. They lead the Motley Few out of town and attempted to take revenge. The party was able to knock one unconscious, and negotiate a temporary ceasefire.
