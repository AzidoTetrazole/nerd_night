The adventure began in the town of Nightstone, and a band of adventurers gathered at the start of a journey that none expected. 

Nightstone was attacked, and giants stole the black stone that gave the city its name. The [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/Ordning|Ordning]] was broken, the ancient hierarchy of the giants tossed aside. [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/People/King Hecaton|King Hecaton]] has gone missing after the murder of his wife [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/People/Queen Neri|Queen Neri]]. [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/People/Princess Sarissa|Princess Sarissa]] is left on the throne while giants rampage across the Sword Coast.

The Motley Few discovered that Queen Neri was killed in a plot by the [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/People/Kracken Society|Kraken Society]], then King Hecaton was held on a ship in the ocean. Meanwhile, [[Previous Campaigns/Motley Few/People/Imryth|Imryth]] had disguised herself as a giant and was sewing discord.

The Motley Few managed to free King Hecaton from his captivity. Upon his return, Imryth retreated to her lair. The Motley Few joined the giants in assaulting her lair!

With the defeat of Imryth, the Giants and "Small folk" entered a new age of cooperation.
