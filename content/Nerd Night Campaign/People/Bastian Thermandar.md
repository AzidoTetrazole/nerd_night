A Fire Genasi member of the [[Nerd Night Campaign/People/Cult of the Eternal Flame|Cult of the Eternal Flame]]. He is claimed to be at [[Nerd Night Campaign/Places/Scarlet Moon Hall|Scarlet Moon Hall]]. He worked with the [[Nerd Night Campaign/People/Bruldenthar|Bruldenthar]] and [[Nerd Night Campaign/People/Rhundorth|Rhundorth]] of the Harpers to arrange the caravan from Mirabar.

[[[Axel]] revealed that Bastian was involved in the deaths of his family, and Axel is seeking revenge!
