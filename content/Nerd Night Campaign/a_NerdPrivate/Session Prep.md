### The Hexalogies of the Plates
1. **The Truth of the Perimeter:** Just as a circle has no beginning and no end, **Justice** must be applied to all equally, without hierarchy or edge.
2. **The Equilibrium of the Scales:** Life is a balanced **Plate**. To take without giving is to tilt the world into chaos; to give without receiving is to empty the vessel.
3. **The Mirror’s Reflection:** Every plate is a polished surface. What you see in others is merely the **Reflection** of your own internal geometry.
4. **The Strength of the Circle:**  We are made stronger by our collective effort.
5. **The Debt of the Diameter:** True **Justice** requires looking across the circle to the person furthest from you and recognizing that you are connected by the same center.
6. **The Final Orbit:** At the end of all things, we do not disappear; we simply complete the **Circle**, returning our energy to the great resonance from which it was first struck.

A circle is defined by its center.
