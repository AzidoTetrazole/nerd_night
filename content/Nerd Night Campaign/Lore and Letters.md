## Letter to Thurl Merosska

A letter found in Feathergale Spire, during a hurried escape.

"Merosska, We are pleased to hear about the outcome of your altercation with the Black Earth cult, and we praise you for the capture of one of their prisoners. This noblewoman from Waterdeep has an interesting tale to tell, and we shall enjoy interrogating her further. Keep a close watch on the [[Nerd Night Campaign/Places/Sacred Stone Monastery|Sacred Stone Monastery]]. I want to know what our enemy is planning next. Your beloved queen, Aerisi Kalinoth"
