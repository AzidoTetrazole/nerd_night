## Profile
Meanie is a soft spoken warlock of some celestial being. He developed his powers after a disaster in his hometown that caused great flooding, where he and his sister rescued as many as he could. After discovering his power, he hid it from all who knew him (including his parents). He has been on the lookout for a unicorn?
