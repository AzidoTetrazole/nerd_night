## DM Notes

Connections:
* Zigbar - trainer - Member of [[Barakus|Barakus]] rogue crew
* [[Bastian Thermandar - DM Notes]] - Member of Eternal Flame
	* Stole the dagger "Tinderstrike" from Axel's father. Tinderstrike is the Roanona family "Legacy"
	* Fire Genasi
	* Suspicious, ambitious

The barony of Roanona was just outside of Nesme, near the Evermoors in northern Faerun. Like the other Barons of Nesme, metalworking and weaponcrafting were the primary form of trade.  You met Bastian Thermandar when he came to your family home. He is a Fire Genasi with flaming red hair, and burning embers for eyes.  He ingratiated himself to your father, offering to help him improve the metals and weapons being created by the Roanona's.

One day, after returning from hunting on the family estate, Axel found his home in flames and the family hanging from the walls. Bloodied and on the brink of death his father sees him and yells out to "Protect the family legacy! Avenge our family!" 

A mob of villiagers arrives, blaming Axel for the murder of his family. Despite his protests, the villagers are convinced he is responsible. As Axel flees for his life, he looks one last time at his burning home. He sees Bastian standing in the burning doorway, smiling, before he turns and walks into the flame of the burning home.

Axel lives in poverty for some time. Being desperate he steals only what he needs to survive, but is caught stealing from the wrong person. Zigbar trains Axel.

After your training with Zigbar, you have been on the trail of Bastian, tracking him to the Dessarin Valley.
* Was trained by Zigbar for at least 3 years
